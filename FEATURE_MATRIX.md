| Required heading + route sequencing (planner/follow) | — | `src/flightPlanCore.js`, `src/followFlight.js`, `src/flightPlanner.js`, `tools/verify-flight-planner.mjs` | — | **Fixed** (ATASOFT). Two defects, both in the shared maths. (1) `bearingDeg()` computed `atan2(dx, dy)`, while every other bearing in the build (the radar's own HDG tool, the measuring line, Centre mode) uses `atan2(dx, -dy)` - y grows DOWNWARD on the map, so north is -y. Every required heading was therefore mirrored about the east-west axis: on a live IRFD->KNIFE leg the HUD said 173° where the waypoint lay at 007°, the turn was instructed the wrong way, and a pilot who followed it swung away from the waypoint and back (the "it zig-zags instead of pointing straight at the waypoint" report). (2) Waypoint progression compared the distance to a waypoint and a perpendicular through it, which cannot sequence a corner that was cut, a route that doubles back, a leg shorter than the waypoint radius, or a cursor left behind by a reload - and it read a leg with no coordinates as "not passed", freezing the route on a waypoint the flight had already flown. It is now route-progress sequencing: the leg the aircraft is on is the route segment it is closest to, and the waypoint still ahead is where that segment ends, forward-only, bounded by a catch-up allowance per update and gated on being near the route at all. |

## Not carried over from 24RC (research-only repo)

24RC (HotDog640, GPL-3.0) was inspected but is architecturally a different
stack (TypeScript/React/PixiJS) with a smaller feature set at review time
(no split-screen, ground view, pinning, or measuring tool). No code from it
was merged into this build; see `ATTRIBUTION.md`.
