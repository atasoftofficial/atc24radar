Free · No account · No download

Your job, with a better radar.

ATC24 Radar by ATASOFT is a free browser radar for PTFS / ATC24 controllers. Open a tab, pick your field, and work the traffic - nothing to install, nothing to sign up for, no paid tier hiding the useful parts. It is a community project, and it is meant to make a controller's shift easier.

  • Your board fills itself. Filed departures and arrivals appear before the aircraft is even airborne, and the fields the system already knows - type, destination, origin, filed level - are written in for you.
  • The next controller sees your clearance. Squawk, cleared level, runway, SID, stand and the “cleared” tick are shared between the controllers working that flight, so nobody is guessing what the previous position issued.
  • Level check at a glance. A flight sitting on its level is marked automatically (±200 ft), so a glance across a busy board tells you who is level and who is not.
  • ATIS without alt-tabbing. Read every published report on the network, and load a field's runways and QNH straight into the ATIS generator.
  • Everything else a controller asks for: live radar with trails and altitude colouring, ground view, split screen, airport charts, approach and centreline tools, Centre mode, emergency and traffic alerts, and a measuring tool.

Open the radar Free to use. Unofficial and not affiliated with PTFS/ATC24 or Roblox.

What this is

ATC24 Radar is a radar client for Roblox Pilot Training Flight Simulator (PTFS) / ATC24. It is an independent community project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Roblox Corporation, the PTFS/ATC24 development team, or any airline referenced in aircraft callsign data.

The aircraft symbols on the scope are real top-view aircraft drawings, one single-piece SVG per aircraft type, from RexKramer1/AircraftShapesSVG (GPL-3.0). The files ship with this build in public/assets/aircraft-shapes/ and are baked into the scope by tools/build-aircraft-shapes.mjs; the scope's size, orientation and data-block offsets are derived from those drawings. Where a type has no drawing of its own, its associated note in that file names the real aircraft of the same class that stands in.

How the data works

Aircraft positions are polled from ATC24's public endpoint (https://24data.ptfs.app/acft-data/event) by a small Node.js backend every few seconds. Flight plans arrive separately over ATC24's WebSocket feed (wss://24data.ptfs.app/wss) and are matched to aircraft by Roblox player name. A flight status (climbing / cruising / descending / taxiing / landed / parked / no flight plan) is computed locally from altitude, ground speed and flight-plan cruise altitude — it is a heuristic, not a value provided directly by ATC24. Fields ATC24 does not provide (such as controller/sector assignment) are not shown or invented.
