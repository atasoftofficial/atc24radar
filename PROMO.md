## 3. Long ad (forum / Reddit / Discord announcement)

> **If you control on PTFS / ATC24, this might make your next session easier — and it's free.**
>
> ATC24 Radar by ATASOFT is a browser radar client for PTFS controllers. Open the
> link, pick the field you're working, and you're online. No download, no account, no
> premium tier — everything is there from the first second.
>
> **What it actually does for you**
>
> - **Departure and arrival boards that fill themselves.** Filed plans come from the
>   network's own lists, so your board shows an inbound before the aircraft is even in
>   the air. Type, destination, origin and filed level are already written in.
> - **Clearances that travel.** The squawk you issue, the level you clear a flight at,
>   the runway, the SID, the stand and the "cleared" tick are shared between the
>   controllers working that flight. The arrival controller sees what the departure
>   controller issued instead of guessing.
> - **Level check at a glance.** A flight sitting on its level (±200 ft) is marked, so
>   one look across a busy board tells you who is level and who is still climbing.
> - **ATIS, read-only, all fields.** Every published report on the network, with the
>   publishing controller and the full text. One button loads its runways and QNH into
>   the ATIS generator.
> - **The rest of the toolkit:** live radar with full-flight trails, ground view, split
>   screen, airport charts, approach/centreline tools, Centre (en-route) mode,
>   emergency and traffic alerts, and a measuring tool.
>
> It's a community project — written by controllers, for controllers, running on
> community data feeds. Free to use, free to share.
>
> 👉 **https://atc24radar.atasoft.xyz**
